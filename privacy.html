Privacy and visitor statistics

Local saves

Saved opportunities, personal filters and application tracking notes use this browser's local storage. They are not automatically transferred between devices. A manual backup can be downloaded.

Recorded site visits

The homepage optionally requests a public counter from Abacus. This is a count of recorded page visits, not unique people, sales or verified human visitors. It increments at most once per browser tab session when available. The counter service receives the request and may process network details such as IP address under its own policy. If unavailable, the counter displays a dash instead of an invented number.

External sources

Official-source links open other websites with their own privacy and eligibility policies. Always inspect the organizer's terms before participating.

No customer accounts

This static site does not provide secure accounts or take payments. Do not enter personal secrets into local notes.

Important: verify before applying

Programs and offers can change or close. This is a source-linked research catalogue, not a claims or guarantees service. Always check the original organizer's terms, eligibility, fees and deadlines.
