How to discover a real hidden opportunity

1. Explore a department

Choose an interest such as product testing, travel privileges, paid research or hidden money. Filter by country and read eligibility conditions.

2. Check the original source

Our catalogue collects public original links; it does not guarantee that an opportunity remains active. A program may be invitation-only, restricted or closed.

3. Save and track privately

Saved entries and progress notes remain in your browser. Use Save Backup or Export before switching devices.

4. Avoid scams

Do not share passwords or bank information with unknown third parties. Official unclaimed-money claims are typically free. Verify any requested fees independently.

Important: verify before applying

Programs and offers can change or close. This is a source-linked research catalogue, not a claims or guarantees service. Always check the original organizer's terms, eligibility, fees and deadlines.
